I added a [Five Elements interactive reference](/tcm/Five-Elements-Interactive-Reference.html) to my [Volume 1 quick reference](/tcm/Essentials-of-Chinese-Medicine-Volume-1-Quick-Reference.html). It brings the correspondences together in a wheel and table, with options to explore the relationships and switch between English, Traditional Chinese, and Simplified Chinese labels.

It's based on Table 1.5, Figure 1.4, and the discussion of Five Elements relationships in [*Essentials of Chinese Medicine: Vol. 1. Foundations of Chinese Medicine*](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-84882-112-5), edited by Zhanwen Liu (Springer, 2010).
