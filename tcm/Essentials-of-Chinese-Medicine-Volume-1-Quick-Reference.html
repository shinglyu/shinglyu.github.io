Essentials of Chinese Medicine, Volume 1 — Quick Reference

This chapter-by-chapter reference covers the book's indexed TCM concepts and diagnostic and therapeutic vocabulary, together with its named clinical signs and conditions. Page ranges are the book's printed page numbers. Terms are grouped where they are principally introduced; recurring terms may appear in more than one chapter when their context differs. Terms from the book are shown in Traditional Chinese. For clinical terms, a common-language equivalent is included in parentheses where useful.

Core TCM terms were cross-checked against the supplied Chinese classics reference; medical conditions use standard clinical translations. The WHO column lists the standard English term, its Simplified Chinese form, and the publication's printed page number. Names of TCM organs refer to functional systems and are not always identical to the corresponding anatomical organs.
